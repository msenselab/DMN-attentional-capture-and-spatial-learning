# Intact-DMN supportive CPM: capture and biased-dimension spatial learning

Authorized exploratory extension after earlier network analyses. This is not preregistered, not outcome-blind independent confirmation, and not a replacement for prior correction families/results. All writes confined to this new directory inside cpm; preserve old files and immutable sources. This analysis tests supportive same-cohort held-out prediction, not network uniqueness, mechanism, or independent external validation.

## Inputs and targets
Exact N33 common399 scanner matrices (SS16/DS17), uniform native323 omission; corrected primary nuisance pipeline, physical Yeo7 intact Default91. All 4,095 within-DMN upper-triangle Fisher-z connections are candidate features. Do not substitute Yeo17 ABC79 or historical neural trial columns; no new exclusions, imputation, edge preselection from full-sample behavior, threshold tuning or atlas changes.
Reconstruct from immutable 10,995 correct scanner trials (RT strictly >200 and <=3000ms; initial trials included; both runs pooled), 6,721 biased trials:
- capture50_ms = 0.5(mean biased Frequent RT + mean biased Rare RT) − mean Absent RT.
- learning_ms = mean biased Rare RT − mean biased Frequent RT.
Biased dimension is orientation in SS and color in DS. Validate against existing equal-region participant companion and frozen learning targets. Spatial learning here is a cross-sectional Rare−Frequent difference, not a fitted within-condition time slope.

## Six fixed CPMs
Archive order: capture50 Pooled, SS, DS; learning Pooled, SS, DS. Pooled training uses 32 participants in each leave-one-participant-out fold. SS models train only within SS (15 training participants); DS models only within DS (16). Subgroup performance is NOT a subset of pooled-model predictions.
For every fold, derive target group means only from training participants; center training targets by their group means and held-out target by that held-out participant's training-group mean. Subgroup centering uses its sole group's training mean. Screen raw individual edges using ordinary two-sided Pearson p<.01 against the training-centered target: pooled training df30, SS df13, DS df14. Neural edges are not group-centered. Signed strength=sum positive selected raw edges minus sum negative selected raw edges. Fit training-only OLS intercept plus signed strength and predict held-out target in centered ms. Zero/constant signed strength falls back to centered prediction zero (group-mean baseline in raw units). If final predictions are constant store r=0 as a flagged fallback, not a mathematically defined Pearson coefficient.
Evaluate signed Pearson r of all held-out predictions versus fold-centered held-out targets; negative r is unfavorable prediction. Also save RMSE and centered-zero baseline RMSE descriptively, without a second inferential family. Target centering, screening and OLS refit in all null/resampling draws.

## Prediction inference
10,000 identical shared within-group label maps across outcomes, seed20261004; numpy default_rng, permutation of DS then SS per draw. Repeat complete LOOCV for each of all six models. Within-group models use maps restricted to their participant identities. Primary two-sided equal-tail p=min(1,2 min[(upper+1)/10001,(lower+1)/10001]), Holm across all six models. Absolute-r tail is a sensitivity with its own full Holm-6; report both even when decisions disagree. No ordinary correlation p or naive participant-independent Fisher interval for LOOCV predictions.
The pooled learning model exactly replicates the earlier intact Yeo7 DMN candidate, not an independent new test. Reuse exact earlier seed/maps and verify observed predictions and every pooled learning permutation r against its archived DMN column. This new six-model family does not erase the old Holm-7 inference.

## Full-pipeline uncertainty and group comparisons
5,000 paired stratified participant-bootstrap multiplicity weights, seed20261006, DS17 then SS16 sampled with replacement in each draw; identical draws across all six models. Exclude ALL copies of the held-out identity from each training fold; weighted training centering, weighted edge correlation/screen, weighted signed-strength OLS, and weighted held-out r refit throughout. Screening degrees of freedom are replication-equivalent sum of training weights minus two; fewer unique subjects is a bootstrap limitation, not leakage. Fail rather than silently discard a draw with fewer than two distinct identities in a group or constant training target. Record all draws and degeneracy flags.
Save percentile 95% full-pipeline r intervals, explicitly approximate. For each outcome, delta = r_SS-only − r_DS-only; bootstrap both group pipelines to form paired delta intervals and approximate centered-bootstrap two-sided p=(1+count(|delta_boot−delta_obs|>=|delta_obs|))/5001, Holm across two group-comparison tests. These are not exact randomization tests; groups have different assigned physical dimensions. Test joint two-outcome group-difference vector first using observed T=delta' Sigma^+ delta, Sigma=bootstrap covariance, centered-bootstrap null T_b=(delta_b−delta)' Sigma^+(delta_b−delta), add-one upper-tail p; save covariance, rank, statistic and chi-square sensitivity. A subgroup threshold crossing but not the other's does not establish group moderation; null moderation does not establish equivalence. Subgroup prediction outputs are reported regardless, as directly requested.
